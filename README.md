# Dental Clarity: Bridging the Gap Between Dental Care and Patient Understanding

Dental Clarity is a web-based patient education application designed to help patients better understand their dental diagnoses, recommended treatments, and aftercare instructions. The application translates complex dental terminology into simple, easy-to-understand explanations and provides educational visuals to help patients understand their oral health. Dental professionals can create patient profiles, select diagnoses, develop personalized treatment plans, and provide aftercare instructions. Dental Clarity also supports English and Spanish translations to make dental information more accessible. Patients can receive personalized appointment summaries by email, allowing them to review important information after leaving the dental office.

## Live Site:
https://dental-clarityrc.onrender.com/


## Features

**Patient Profiles:** Create and manage patient profiles containing patient names, email addresses, and preferred languages.

**Professional Dashboard:** View patient information and navigate between patient profiles, diagnoses, treatment plans, and appointment summaries.

**Diagnosis Library:** Select from dental diagnoses such as gingivitis, periodontal disease, and dental caries.

**Simplified Diagnoses:** Translate complex dental terminology into clear, patient-friendly explanations that describe the condition, its causes, and why treatment may be necessary.

**Visual Education:** Display educational images comparing healthy oral structures with dental conditions to help patients better understand their diagnoses.

**Language Translation:** Switch between English and Spanish to make dental explanations, treatment information, and aftercare instructions more accessible.

**Personalized Treatment Plans:** Create treatment plans based on each patient's diagnosis and recommended dental procedures.

**Aftercare Instructions:** Provide clear instructions to help patients understand how to care for their oral health following treatment.

**Appointment Summaries:** Generate personalized visit summaries containing patient diagnoses, treatment plans, and aftercare instructions.

**Email Summaries:** Send patients their appointment summaries by email so they can easily review their dental information at home.

**Patient Record Management:** Create, view, update, and delete patient information, diagnoses, treatment plans, and appointment summaries as appropriate.

## Tech Stack

Frontend: EJS, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap  
Backend: Node.js, Express.js  
Database: MongoDB Atlas with Mongoose  
Authentication: Passport.js

## Installation

1. Clone repo.
2. Run `npm install`.
3. Configure the required environment variables for MongoDB, authentication, and email functionality.

## Usage

1. Run `node server.js`.
2. Navigate to `localhost:8080`.
3. Create an account or log in to access the professional dashboard.
4. Create a patient profile and select the patient's preferred language.
5. Select a dental diagnosis to generate a patient-friendly explanation.
6. Explore educational visuals and create a personalized treatment plan with aftercare instructions.
7. Generate an appointment summary and send it to the patient by email.

## Project Inspiration

As a former dental hygienist, I saw firsthand how patients often leave dental appointments without fully understanding their diagnoses, recommended treatments, or aftercare instructions. Dental Clarity combines my clinical experience with my software engineering skills to help bridge the communication gap between dental professionals and patients. The goal is to make dental education more understandable, accessible, and patient-centered, empowering patients to feel more confident and informed about their oral health.
